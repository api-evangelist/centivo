Primary care was meant to be the “quarterback” of the healthcare system — a trusted partner guiding patients, driving early intervention and prevention, ensuring access to the affordable care people need. It was the place where care began, relationships were built and long-term health was nurtured. But somewhere along the way, that vision got lost.
